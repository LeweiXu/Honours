Why multi-page is a different problem

An Evidence-Management Problem

What the document does to the model
Exceeds the context window total length outruns the backbone
Evidence sparse & scattered a few pages carry the answer
Structure spans pages hierarchy, cross-references, tables
Long document p1 … pN
What the system does back
Retrieve relevant pages rank and pass a compact subset
Compress / sample fit a fixed token budget
Preserve visual fidelity keep what text extraction loses
The system must decide what to retain, discard, retrieve, or acquire.
