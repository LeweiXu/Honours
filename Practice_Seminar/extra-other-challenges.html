Other Challenges

System capabilitythree of five
Evidence pipeline High-resolution page reading and document-scale aggregation exceed any one budget. Every family buys the trade with a different loss.
Faithfulness & abstention When retrieval misses, systems still answer confidently. Few abstain, report calibrated uncertainty, or cite the page.
Efficiency & deployability Agentic pipelines issue tens of LLM calls per question, yet latency and cost curves are almost never reported.
Data & evaluationtwo of five
Supervision scarcity & contamination Per-page annotation does not scale, so supervision is LVLM-synthesised, and benchmarks are built from the same small pool of PDFs that fine-tuning draws on.
Evaluation methodology & scope Answer-only accuracy hides retrieval failure and rewards right answers reached by wrong reasoning. Task, language and domain scope stay narrow.
