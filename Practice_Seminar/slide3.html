Multi-page multimodal representation

What Makes Multi-Page Hard?

Every evidence decision is enacted over a modality. Three axes, three sets of pressures.
Text§4.1
Reading everything is impossible; every fix is lossy somewhere.
Volume
length outruns the context
Continuity
paragraphs and tables break at page edges
Noise
OCR error accumulates across pages
Heterogeneity
text density varies page to page
Visual§4.2
Pixels keep what text extraction loses, but they cost tokens.
Resolution
cannot hold full fidelity at document length
Signal competition
text, figures and layout share one budget
Heterogeneity
visual complexity varies page to page
Structure§4.3
What turns isolated pages into one document.
Hierarchy
sections and reading order span the whole file
Cross-page references
“see Figure 3” points somewhere else
Spanning elements
tables and paragraphs continue across pages
Cross-cutting

Multi-hop reasoning: the answer is assembled from pages that are far apart, so no single page scores well on its own.
