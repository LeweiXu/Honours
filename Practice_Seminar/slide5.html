What to notice

Accuracy climbs with richness and peaks at TLV, 31.9 to 52.5, but the ladder is not monotonic: dropping text for the page image alone costs 6.9 points. Richer rungs also absorb parser quality, with the spread between parsers falling from 13.6 points at TL to 5.1 at TLV. The shape holds across every domain and evidence source; only the height moves.
