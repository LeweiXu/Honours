% Verdict flips (\%) when $k$ non-evidence pages are added on top of a question's full gold set, measured within-question against the same question's oracle page set at the combined encoding, colqwen3 ranking. Columns and shading are as in Table~\ref{tab:selection_withholding} and on the same scales, so the two tables can be read against each other. Each block holds the gold-page count fixed and reads against its own $k{=}0$ oracle, so blocks are not comparable to one another. $^{\dagger}$ marks a row over fewer than 30 questions, where a single question moves the percentage by more than three points; the three-gold block is thin at every source, which is why the figure drops it. Adding pages breaks under a tenth of correct answers and repairs almost as many as it breaks, so the net effect stays near zero even at three added pages, against the one-way loss of withholding.\par}
