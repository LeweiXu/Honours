Representation Experiments

Two questions about the page itself: what can an input express, and how much is lost converting the page into it.

Modality ceiling

What the input can express

Four rungs of increasing richness. T is the PDF's embedded text layer. TL is layout-aware parser markdown. TLV adds the rendered page image. V is the page image alone.

The comparison asks whether the required evidence is still recoverable once the textual, structural, or visual signal is taken away, rather than assuming one representation is best everywhere.

Conversion fidelity

How much survives the conversion

Hold the rung fixed and vary the quality of each channel instead: the parser for text, across PyMuPDF, MinerU, PaddleOCR-VL and Unlimited-OCR, and the render resolution for the image.

This separates what a modality cannot carry in principle from what a bad conversion destroyed in practice.

The annotated evidence pages are supplied in both, so retrieval is not a variable here. Whatever changes is the encoding.
