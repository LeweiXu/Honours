Retrieve broadlyCoverage first: a missed page cannot be recovered later.
Filter or rerankControl what actually reaches the model.
Judge the setIs the whole set sufficient? If not, retrieve again.

A strong visual retriever pays off on collections queried often. A light text retriever suits tight budgets. Judge by final answers and latency, not recall alone.
