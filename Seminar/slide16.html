Datasets

Datasets serve three roles, and the multi-page setting makes all three harder: per-page annotation does not scale to documents of tens or hundreds of pages, and long PDFs are harder to source and license than single pages.

Pretraining

The exception rather than the norm. Genuinely multi-page corpora have appeared only recently, so most systems inherit a backbone pretrained on single pages and skip the stage entirely.

Rare

Fine-tuning

Where the weight falls. Broad instruction mixes of released question-answer collections, plus bespoke corpora synthesised by a strong model over scraped PDFs.

Mostly synthetic

Benchmarking

Accumulated in tiers, from single-page extraction through to long documents whose evidence spans pages. In practice evaluation has consolidated on a small, much-reused pool.

Narrow pool

Benchmarks measure whether the answer was right. For this problem we also want to know whether the right evidence was found and used to get there.
