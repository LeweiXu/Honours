A Plausible Answer Does Not Mean the Evidence Is Complete

Selection
1

Retrieved evidence can look sufficient

A page may support a reasonable answer while an unseen page adds an exception, a qualification, or another fact that changes it. Producing an answer is not proof that retrieval is complete.

2

Retrieving more creates another risk

Missing a required page is damaging. But adding unnecessary pages also lowers accuracy.

3

Aim for complete, focused evidence

Keep all the necessary context while limiting distraction and cost. Stop when the question’s requirements are covered, not when an answer can be produced.
