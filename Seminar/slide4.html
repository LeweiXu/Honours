What is Document Understanding?

One broad way to define it: get a system to answer a natural-language question over a document, accurately. The systems range from rule-based lookup, through trained vision-language models, to pipelines built around pretrained general-domain ones.

1

“What year was this report published?”

fact lookup
2

“According to the table, which region had the highest revenue in 2023?”

read a table
3

“How much did the leading region's revenue grow between the 2022 and 2023 results tables?”

two tables, then arithmetic
4

“Does the growth described in the executive summary agree with the figures in the results table?”

prose against numbers
5

“Across the report and its appendix, what evidence supports the conclusion that the new method improved performance, and what limitation qualifies it?”

gather, then qualify
Illustrative questions, not quoted benchmark items
