Single-Page VRDU Overview

The single-page setting is largely solved, and multi-page systems inherit their per-page machinery from it.

OCR-dependent and OCR-free architectures for MLLM-based VRDU. Adapted from Ding et al.

OCR-dependent

Parse the page first, recovering text with its bounding-box coordinates. Captures structure without heavy visual pretraining, but ties accuracy to the parser's.

OCR-free

A vision encoder reads the rendered page from pixels. Handles handwriting and irregular layouts, but needs high resolution, so compression usually follows.
