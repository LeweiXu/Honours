Reasoning Experiments

With representation and selection controlled, what remains is the reasoner. Two different problems, so they are tested separately.

Evidence integration

Can it combine what it has?

Split the questions by the structure of the evidence they need: one page and one source, several sources on one page, or evidence distributed across pages.

Then add a step-by-step reasoning prompt and see which of those groups it actually helps. The annotated pages are supplied throughout, so any deficit arises after the evidence has arrived.

Response calibration

Should it answer at all?

Vary both whether the evidence supports an answer and whether the model is explicitly permitted to abstain.

Answerable questions get their oracle gold pages. Unanswerable ones get the three top-ranked pages by BM25: plausible context that does not support an answer. That measures unsupported answers in one direction and false refusals in the other.

Everything is decoded greedily, so a condition is reproducible from its identity rather than from a seed.
