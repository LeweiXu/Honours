Abstention Is Not Well Calibrated

The last reasoning decision is whether the evidence supports an answer at all. An abstention instruction makes the model more cautious, but not more discerning.

Effect of targeted abstention prompting at the TLV rung

Reading it

It works where it should. On unanswerable questions, refusal rises from 45.5% to 74.6%, cutting unsupported answers substantially.

And it fires where it should not. On answerable questions, refusal rises from 6.2% to 27.7%, and correct answers fall from 49.5% to 40.0%.

Difficulty is being read as insufficiency. Under the instruction, refusal reaches 35.5% for figure questions and 30.3% for tables, against 24.1% for text — with oracle pages supplied, so the evidence is definitely there.

The prompt moves willingness, not judgement. It shifts the operating point rather than separating sufficient evidence from insufficient.
