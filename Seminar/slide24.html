Training Strategies

Most systems adopt a backbone already pretrained on single-page data, so fine-tuning carries the weight. Training internalises the inference-time evidence-management decisions into model parameters.

Trained retrieval

Modality-alignment aligns visual page embeddings with OCR-derived text. Cross-page-fusion adds neighbouring-page context to each page representation. Logical-relevance ranks pages by their position in a cross-page reasoning path.

Retriever

Trained reasoning

Trace-imitation supervises the backbone on demonstration chains in a fixed schema. Reward-driven training then refines it with GRPO, rewarding answer accuracy, evidence-page recall, and format compliance.

Reasoner

Trained agentic methods

Trajectory-distillation clones a stronger teacher’s think-action-observation turns through SFT. Reinforcement-learned variants optimise the policy directly against trajectory-level rewards.

Controller

Two techniques recur across all three: parameter-efficient tuning (LoRA, adapters) and multi-stage curricula that go from single-page to document-scale supervision. All of it depends on the available datasets.
