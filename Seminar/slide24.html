Design
  • Match capacity and inference strategy to the operation. Separating answer construction from verification helps, at the cost of extra computation.
Deployment
  • Quantisation keeps accuracy at a smaller footprint but is not faster: under a memory limit prefer a quantised larger model, under a latency limit fewer reasoning steps or a faster backbone.
