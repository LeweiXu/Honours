Selection Experiments

Selection can fail in two opposite ways, and the point of the experiment is that they are not equally damaging. Both start from the pages known to contain the answer.

Evidence coverage

Required evidence removed

Take questions that cite more than one gold page and withhold one of them. Pages are ranked within the document, so we can remove the highest-ranked gold page, the lowest-ranked, or keep only one.

A question that cannot satisfy a condition is excluded and recorded, never quietly satisfied with a different page set.

Distractor exposure

Irrelevant evidence added

Keep the complete gold set and add up to five top-ranked non-gold pages alongside it. Conditions are blocked on the exact gold-page count, so questions needing different amounts of evidence are never pooled.

The supplied context stays at six pages or fewer, so distractor count is not confounded with context length.

The encoding and the reasoner stay fixed across both, so the two effects can be compared directly against the same oracle baseline.
