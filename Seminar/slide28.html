Reasoning Across Pages Remains Hard

Every correct page is supplied, in the richest representation. Cross-page questions are still much harder, and asking for step-by-step reasoning helps only some of them.

Effect of chain-of-thought across evidence structures, TLV rung, oracle evidence pages

Reading it

The deficit is real. On MMLongBench-Doc, one-source questions fall from 66.2% on a single page to 42.0% once the evidence spans pages, and to 35.0% when several sources must be combined as well.

Chain-of-thought helps precisely there. It slightly hurts single-page questions (66.2 to 63.5), and lifts cross-page ones (42.0 to 47.3, and 35.0 to 40.7). The pooled integration deficit narrows from 24.6 to 18.2 points.

Most of the gain is structured evidence. Tables improve by 10.9 points; charts, text and layout by two to three; figures fall slightly.

And it does not generalise. On LongDocURL the same prompt reduces accuracy almost everywhere. More reasoning helps when it matches the task, not because there is more of it.
