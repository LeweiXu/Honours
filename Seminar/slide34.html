Datasets

Datasets serve three roles, and the multi-page setting makes all three harder: per-page annotation does not scale to documents of tens or hundreds of pages, and long PDFs are harder to source and license than single pages.

Pretraining

The exception rather than the norm. Confined to a few page-to-document and MLLM-centric systems that do continued pretraining on an existing backbone, because multi-page corpora are still small.

Rare

Fine-tuning

Has converged on LVLM-synthesised supervision over scraped PDFs, with reinforcement learning increasingly layered on top to shape evidence-grounding and trajectory behaviour.

LVLM-synthesised

Benchmarking

Three tiers: single-page extractive baselines, mid-length multi-page suites, and long-document multimodal benchmarks that need cross-page integration. The same small pool of PDFs gets reused.

Contamination risk

Answer-only accuracy masks retrieval failure and rewards correct answers reached through incorrect reasoning. Newer benchmarks score retrieval and page-localisation as well, but it is not yet standard.
