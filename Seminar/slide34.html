Reading it

Aggregate accuracy barely moves. Single-page questions stay broadly stable, and multi-page questions decline gradually. LongDocURL degrades even less under comparable distractor counts.

But stability is not harmlessness. The verdict transitions show added pages both breaking and repairing answers. Positive and negative effects partly cancel at the aggregate level.

A non-gold page is not always irrelevant. Sometimes it adds a partial instance that makes the model overcount; sometimes it supplies the context that disambiguates the gold pages and repairs the answer.

So lean toward coverage when the later context can absorb it, and control how the retrieved evidence is presented rather than minimising how much of it there is.
