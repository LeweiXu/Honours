Reader, Synthesizer, Arbiter

One underlying model in three roles, differentiated only by the instruction and the input each receives.

reader

Works through the pages in retrieval-ranked order, recording answer-relevant evidence as it goes, and proposes stopping when it thinks it has enough.

synthesizer

Receives the most important pages in full, plus the short records from everything else read, and proposes an answer from that.

arbiter

Accepts the answer, or sends the reader back for more. It cannot write an answer itself, which makes stopping too early recoverable rather than final.

The loop is what makes the amount of reading adapt to the question: an easy one stops early, a hard one keeps going.
