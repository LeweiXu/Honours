Thesis by Compilation

This thesis is structured as a compilation of two papers written during this Honours year, each addressing one of its two aims.

1

Managing Evidence at Document Scale: A Survey of Multi-Page Visually Rich Document Understanding

Lewei Xu, Yihao Ding, Zihan Xu, Siwen Luo, Daochang Liu, Wei Liu
Main Conference of EMNLP 2026 · Chapter 2
Aim 1

Comprehensively survey techniques and architectures for multi-page document understanding, treating it as a problem distinct from the single-page setting.

2

Locating Failure in Multi-Page Visually Rich Document Understanding: An Empirical Attribution

Lewei Xu, Yihao Ding, Zihan Xu, Daniel Yitian Su, Daochang Liu, Siwen Luo, Yifan Peng, Wei Liu
Submitted to EACL, under review · Chapter 3
Aim 2

Attribute MP-VRDU failure to its underlying causes, and resolve the field's competing and largely untested claims about how these systems should be built.
