From Findings to Design

The survey and the experiments both point at design principles. I put them together in a deliberately simple system, to see whether they hold up at the level of a whole pipeline rather than one controlled condition.

Representation

Keep text and images together

Every page read at the full TLV representation, with a strong parser and high-resolution rendering, since the two channels cover for each other's failures.

Selection

Gather broadly, present selectively

Missing evidence is unrecoverable and moderate distraction is not, so read widely in retrieval-ranked order, but carry most of it forward as compact records rather than as full pages.

Reasoning

Check whether the answer is supported

Because the reasoner cannot judge sufficiency on its own, a separate step decides whether to accept the answer or keep reading.

No planner, no tool set, no query reformulation. The point is to validate the principles, not to introduce a complex architecture.
