Three Failure Loci

Three places in the pipeline where an answer can go wrong, each with its own diagnostic question.

Representation

Did we preserve the information the question needs from the page?

Selection

Did that information actually reach the model?

Reasoning

Did the model use it correctly once it had it?

The order matters, and it is what makes attribution possible: we cannot reason over evidence that was lost in encoding, or never selected. So a locus can only be tested once the ones above it are neutralised.
