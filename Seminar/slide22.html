Reasoning Strategies

Reasoning strategies act over a single evidence buffer assembled before the reasoning call: the full document representation, a retrieved subset, or evidence from an agentic step. None updates model parameters.

  • Sequential reasoning: Chain-of-Thought. Extends a single reasoning thread. CoT binds evidence from different pages into one chain and decomposes multi-hop questions into single-hop steps.
  • Sequential reasoning: Self-Reflection. The model evaluates its own draft answer and revises it across rounds, optionally against a verifier or critic.
  • Parallel exploration. Several reasoning paths over the same buffer. Self-Consistency samples multiple chains and takes the majority answer; sampling-adjudication replaces the vote with an LLM judge. Cost scales with the number of samples. Tree-of-Thoughts has seen little uptake.

A chain can only reason over the evidence already assembled. It cannot recover what retrieval missed.
