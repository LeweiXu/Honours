Multi-Page Visually Rich Documents

Meaning is carried jointly by text, vision, and layout rather than by text alone. Multi-page adds a further layer: meaning also runs between pages, as when a claim points to a figure many pages away.

Text. Small, typeset or handwritten, and legible only at far higher input resolution than a typical vision-language task requires.

Vision. Charts, figures, photographs, and handwriting carry meaning that no text extraction recovers.

Layout. Position is semantic, and reading order must be recovered rather than assumed.
