General-Domain MLLMs and VRDU

VRDU long predates general-domain MLLMs; what has changed is that those models now sit at the centre of it. Closed-source assistants have steadily absorbed VRDU techniques into their products, while open-weight models carry the same techniques to documents that cannot leave the premises.

Closed-source products

Three routes cover most of what a consumer assistant does with an uploaded document.

  • Read it whole. Parse the PDF for text, render it to per-page images, and place both in the context window.
  • Read it on demand. The file stays on disk, and tool calls extract or search particular pages.
  • Retrieve over it. Content beyond the context window is indexed and searched rather than ingested.
ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini · NotebookLM

Open-weight models, on premises

Much document work involves material that cannot be sent to a third party at all.

  • Documents that cannot leave. Medical records, legal and financial filings, and mining and resources data.
  • The same pipelines, local weights. Open-weight backbones rebuilt behind the firewall rather than called over an API.
  • Hardware is the binding constraint. Quantisation, retrieval, and page selection do the work the context window cannot.
Qwen3-VL · Gemma · InternVL

Both are document-understanding pipelines. The frontier is how these backbones can be leveraged, together with the machinery built around them, to answer questions over documents they cannot process directly.
