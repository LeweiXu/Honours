Streaming abstention in DocSLM. The document is split into segments. Each segment gives an answer or abstains, and an uncertainty calibrator keeps the answer with the lowest uncertainty. From Hannan et al., CVPR 2026 Findings.
How it works
  • A small vision-language model for long documents under limited memory.
  • Hierarchical multimodal compressor. Each page’s visual, text and layout information is jointly encoded into a fixed 576 tokens.
  • Streaming abstention. The document is split into segments and processed one at a time. Each segment answers or abstains.
  • An uncertainty calibrator keeps the answer with the lowest uncertainty.
