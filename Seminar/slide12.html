How it works
  • Adapts a pretrained multimodal model to read a multi-page document directly, without retrieval.
  • Teaches document-level dependencies through better training data: Doc-750K, about 750,000 question-answer pairs from scientific papers and reviews.
  • InternVL2 is fine-tuned on a data mixture that includes it.
  • At inference, the page images and the question go into the model together, and it answers directly.
