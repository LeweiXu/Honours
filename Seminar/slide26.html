Verdict transitions after withholding or retaining gold evidence pages

Reading it

Withholding one gold page breaks 24.7–27.0% of previously correct answers, while only 4.3–4.5% are repaired. Keeping just one gold page widens it further: 28.4–30.4% break against 2.3–2.6% repaired.

The loss is directional. This is more than the expected correlation between retrieval recall and accuracy. Removing known evidence produces a one-way loss that downstream reasoning rarely reverses.

Which page survives matters too. Keeping the highest-ranked gold page preserves much more than keeping the lowest. Extractive questions tolerate partial evidence; counting and calculation do not.
