How We Isolate a Failure

One simple pipeline throughout, with one condition changed at a time and everything else held fixed.

Encode

Every page becomes text, an image, or both. Done once per document and cached, so no question changes what a page contains.

PaddleOCR-VL · 200 DPI

Retrieve

A retriever scores every encoded page against the question and returns the top k, restored to document order.

ColQwen3 · page-level

Answer

A multimodal model receives those pages and the question, and answers in a single pass. Nothing is fed back.

Qwen3-VL-8B · greedy
  • The stages act once, and in order. That is what lets a failure be attributed rather than guessed at.
  • Gold pages bypass retrieval. For most tests the annotated evidence pages are supplied directly, so selection is neutralised and whatever remains is representation or reasoning.
  • Every reported accuracy carries a confidence interval from a document-level bootstrap, because questions from one document are not independent.
