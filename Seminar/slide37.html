The only multi-page benchmark we know of that annotates, per question, the evidence pages, the kind of evidence it draws on, and the document's domain, while also supplying plausible questions the document cannot answer.

Evidence pages make coverage measurable and define the oracle. Evidence source explains representation results by what the question actually needs. The unanswerable pool makes calibration testable rather than inferred.

LongDocURL

Secondary replication

Longer documents, used to check whether the findings hold under a less demanding setting. Born-digital throughout, almost no unanswerable questions, and many questions answerable from the text even when the annotated evidence is visual.

Its annotated pages are also not a minimal set: several may be redundant or independently sufficient, which we account for when reading its coverage and integration results.

Answers are scored by an LLM judge rather than exact match, since a correct response can differ in units, rounding, or formatting. The judge sees only the question, gold answer, and model answer, never the condition being tested. It agrees with GPT-4o on 96.0% of a stratified sample and with a human annotator on 98.3%. We track refusals and wrong answers separately, not just accuracy.
