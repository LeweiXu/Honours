Reasoning Strategies

Once the evidence is assembled, the model still has to make sense of it. Some answers need facts from different pages combined, step by step.

  • Ask it to show its reasoning. Chain-of-thought elicits the intermediate steps before the answer, which binds evidence from different pages one hop at a time and leaves a trace you can check.
  • Ask it to check itself. Self-reflection has the model audit or revise an initial answer against the same evidence, optionally with a separate verifier.
  • Ask it several times. Sampling multiple reasoning paths and taking the majority buys robustness against a single bad chain, and costs proportionally more compute.

All of it operates over a fixed buffer of evidence assembled before the call. None of it can recover evidence that was never supplied.
