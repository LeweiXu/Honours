How it works
  • Graph-based index. A visual retriever represents each page, and pages are connected by their similarity.
  • Graph traversal. The search starts from pages similar to the question. A small vision-language model helps score relevance, and the top K pages are kept.
  • Question answering. The top K pages go to a separate vision-language model, the reasoner.
