When Is Visual Reading Worth Its Cost?

Representation
1

Vision is expensive

Vision-only needs high-resolution reading, and every page image costs time: 26 s against 1.8 s per question.

2

Text is a cache

Embedded text or a structure-aware parser converts the document once, into a form that is fast at inference time. What is needed depends on the document type and the task.

3

Parsers are moving into vision’s territory

Neural parsers can now express charts and figures as text.

Modality needs differ by domain

T embedded text, TL parsed text, V page image.
