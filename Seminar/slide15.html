Representation: When Is Visual Reading Worth Its Cost?

1

Vision is expensive

At the same 900 input tokens, page images take 21× longer to read than text (22.7 s against 1.1 s a question) and run out of memory on 11% of questions, against 1%.

2

Yet most systems read everything visually

Vision only, or text plus image, for every page. But the best choice depends on the document type and the task: scans have no text layer; born-digital text and tables gain little from the image; charts and figures need it.

3

Modalities are complementary

Text alone 38%, a low-resolution image alone 38%, together 49%. They fail on different questions (only 22% are answered by both), so parser noise and low resolution cover for each other.

4

Deployment: choose, don’t default

A known document type or task fixes the modality: images for scans, cached parser text for reports and tables. Mixed collections: choose per question, as tool-using agents are starting to.
