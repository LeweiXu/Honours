When Is Visual Reading Worth Its Cost?

Representation
1

Visual reading has a cost

Many surveyed systems read page images. Rendering, sending and processing them adds overhead, more so when an agentic workflow inspects pages repeatedly.

2

Text can already be sufficient

Embedded text or a reliable parser may preserve everything a text-based question needs. Vision matters when the answer depends on appearance, layout, or something lost in extraction.

3

Spend the visual budget selectively

Start from what the task requires. Use page images or targeted crops where text cannot reliably preserve the evidence.
