Conversion Quality Matters

A useful input type still fails if the page is converted badly. Parser choice alone moves accuracy by tens of points, so it is not neutral preprocessing.

Reading it

Scans break the text channel entirely. On scanned documents the PDF's embedded text layer is often absent, and accuracy collapses. Conversion failure does not merely degrade the representation, it removes it.

Parsers differ a lot. MinerU is the weakest on both digital and scanned pages; PaddleOCR-VL and Unlimited-OCR recover substantially more.

Vision covers for the parser. Adding the page image compresses the spread between parsers sharply, and the weakest parser benefits most. Many conversion errors are recoverable once a second channel still holds the signal.

Resolution is the mirror image. Raising render resolution helps V far more than TLV, where the text already carries part of what the extra pixels would recover.
