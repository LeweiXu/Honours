Multi-Page Architectural Overview

The survey organises systems by evidence management, into four architectural families. Page-to-document encoding models jointly encode pages; MLLM-centric adaptation models fit multi-page input into an MLLM’s context and token budget; retrieval-augmented pipelines retrieve evidence before generation; adaptive-trajectory pipelines inspect evidence iteratively under an LLM controller.
