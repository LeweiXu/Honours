Does the Document Support the Question in the First Place?

Reasoning
1

Questions can contain unsupported premises

A model may give a fluent, assertive answer even when the document does not support the question. Accepting a false premise and inventing an explanation is a form of hallucination.

2

Task-specific prompts change this behaviour

Explicitly permitting abstention improves rejection of unanswerable questions, but also increases refusal of answerable ones. Prompt refinement has to balance the two.

3

Further reasoning methods are options

Reflexion revises an answer using feedback. Self-consistency samples several reasoning paths and aggregates them. Both add computation, and we did not test them.
