Reasoning: Does the Document Support the Question?

1

Unsupported premises

A model answers fluently even when the document does not support the question. With no instruction it refuses only 45% of unanswerable questions.

“Which YouTube does the slides use…?” The document mentions no video.
✗“A clip from Breaking Bad…” ✓“Not answerable” once it may abstain.

2

Prompts move the trade-off

Permitting abstention lifts refusal to 75%, but also refuses 28% of answerable questions. Reasoning first, then abstaining, wins the accuracy back (51%) and refuses more of the unanswerable (86%).

3

More computation helps, at a price

Self-consistency (40 sampled reasoning paths, majority vote) adds 8 points on answerable questions, at 640 s a question. Reflexion (retry on its own evaluation) adds 14 on unanswerable ones at 47 s, though its self-check passes 94% of its answers.
