Why Locate the Failure?

The survey shows many ways to build these systems. But when a system answers wrongly, different papers give different explanations for why, and those explanations imply different fixes.

“How much did the leading region's revenue grow between the 2022 and 2023 tables?”
The system answers 14%. The correct answer is 23%. What went wrong?

The page was read badly

The parser garbled a figure in the table, or the page was rendered too small to read it.

The right page was missed

Only one of the two tables was retrieved, so the comparison was never possible.

The model could not use it

Both tables were present and legible, and it still picked the wrong row or did the arithmetic wrong.

End-to-end accuracy cannot tell these apart, and under a fixed compute budget you have to know which one to spend on.
