Real-World Constraints

Assistants are one application. In industry: one or a few specific tasks, over thousands to millions of documents.

HealthcareExtract fields from scanned patient records and referrals.
Mining and resourcesClassify and summarise technical and safety reports.
FinancePull figures from annual reports and filings.
LegalFind the relevant clauses across contracts.

Privacy

Privacy or data-residency requirements may rule out a third-party service.

Cost

Even without privacy concerns, a commercial API is very expensive at this volume.

Scale

Uploading documents one by one to an assistant is not feasible. You may also need structured output for another system.

API or local model, you still have to implement and deploy your own system, tailored to your task, documents and output. API access does not give you the workflow behind the consumer assistant.
