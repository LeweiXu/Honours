Challenges of the Multi-Page Setting

1

The document exceeds the context window

Long multi-page documents routinely outrun what a backbone can hold, so the system must select which parts of the document reach the MLLM at all.

2

Scaling the context does not resolve it

Long-context models attend poorly to evidence buried mid-input, and memory and compute grow with sequence length, so the achievable context is bounded by hardware.

3

Every required evidence unit must be located

Answering often means integrating evidence from several parts of the document at once, and missing any one of them is enough to answer incorrectly or not at all.

4

Cross-page structure is not recoverable in isolation

Section hierarchy and cross-references cannot be read off any single part of the document, yet questions often need them to gather the evidence in the first place.
