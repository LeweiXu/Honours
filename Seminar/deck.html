Outline

What this talk covers

20 minutes
Four parts
01Background

Why multi-page is a different problem

What a visually rich document is, and why the single-page setting does not simply scale up to many pages.

  • Text, layout and pixels together
  • Evidence sparse and scattered
  • The system must choose what to read
02Literature

Organising the field

The review reads MP-VRDU as an evidence-management problem, sorted by where that evidence is handled.

  • Four architectural families
  • Text, visual, cross-page structure
  • Every family relocates the bottleneck
03Empirical study

Locating the failure

The bulk of the talk. One pipeline, one stage moved at a time, so failure can be pinned to a stage.

  • Representation, selection, reasoning
  • Two benchmarks, one pipeline
  • What fails, and what does not
04Conclusion

Where this leads

A pipeline built straight out of the findings, and then the challenges that remain open.

  • A simple adaptive-trajectory method
  • What the thesis settles
  • What it does not
