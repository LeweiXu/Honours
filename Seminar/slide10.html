Visual Modality

Page images preserve what a text extract misses: charts, handwriting, fine layout, and visual relationships. What they cost is tokens.

  • Resolution. Token cost scales with page count multiplied by per-page resolution, and fine-grained content is only legible at the high end of that range.
  • So: compress, or select. Keep every page in context at reduced fidelity, or read a retrieved subset at full fidelity. The first blurs small text and chart annotations; the second never sees a page the retriever missed.
  • Signal competition. Within one page, the same visual budget has to carry text-bearing pixels, figures and charts, and layout cues at once.

The trade is always the same: broad page coverage against readable detail. Beyond text and images, a document also has relationships that connect its pages.
