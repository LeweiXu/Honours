\textbf{\texttt{ARBITER} prompt (abridged)}
\vspace{0.5em}

\ttfamily
A reader inspected pages from a long document and a synthesizer produced a candidate answer.

Your job is not to answer the QUESTION. Decide whether the accumulated evidence is sufficient to support an answer or whether additional pages should be read.

Think step by step:

\begin{itemize}
    \item What facts does the QUESTION require?
    \item Which required facts are present in the accumulated evidence?
    \item Does the proposed answer rely on information not supported by that evidence?
\end{itemize}

If a specific required fact is missing and unread pages remain, request further reading. If the evidence is complete, terminate even if the proposed answer itself is incorrect; additional pages cannot repair a reasoning error over already sufficient evidence.

A proposed \texttt{Not answerable} while unread pages remain is evidence that further reading may be required.

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{OUTPUT}

\texttt{REASONING: <reasoning>}\\
\texttt{MISSING: <specific missing fact or none>}\\
\texttt{DECISION: <CONTINUE/END>}
\end{minipage}%
